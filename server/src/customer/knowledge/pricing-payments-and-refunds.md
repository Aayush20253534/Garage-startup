# Pricing, Payments And Refund Help

> Customer-assistant knowledge verified on 25 July 2026.

## Estimated Service Price

A service page may show an approved estimated price or price range for the selected city, vehicle, and service. This is a planning estimate, not a guaranteed final repair quotation. The final service amount depends on the work actually completed and is confirmed through the booking flow.

If the app says that a price is not allocated for the selected vehicle or location, the assistant must not guess a value. The customer should change the vehicle or city selection, choose another available service, or try again after pricing becomes available.

## Platform Fee At Checkout

The online checkout amount is the Rovauto platform fee. It is separate from the final service amount paid to the garage after the work is completed.

Available customer wallet balance may be applied to the platform fee. When the wallet does not cover the complete platform fee, the remaining amount is paid through the supported payment screen. When the wallet covers the full fee, an external payment page may not be required.

## Pending Or Unclear Payment

If a booking remains in **Pending Payment**, open Pending Bookings and use the payment action shown there. Do not repeatedly pay from multiple tabs or create duplicate bookings while a payment result is still being confirmed.

Use the payment history, booking details, and wallet history as the source of truth. The chatbot cannot inspect card or bank credentials, manually confirm a payment, alter a transaction, or guarantee when a provider will finish processing it.

## Final Garage Payment

The final service amount is paid directly to the garage after the completed work is reviewed. For pickup-and-delivery bookings, this is handled during delivery confirmation. For self drop-off bookings, it is handled when the customer returns to the garage and confirms vehicle collection.

The customer should verify the completed work and amount before confirming delivery or collection. The chatbot must not invent discounts, add-on prices, or a final bill.

## Cancellation Credits And Refund Questions

When an eligible paid booking is cancelled before service begins, the paid platform-fee amount may be credited to the customer's Rovauto wallet according to the booking record. Check wallet and payment history to confirm the result.

A wallet credit is not the same as a guaranteed immediate bank or card refund. For missing, duplicated, or disputed transactions, create a support ticket linked to the booking or payment so the support team can review the recorded transaction.

## Payment Safety

Never send card numbers, bank details, UPI PINs, CVVs, passwords, OTPs, payment links, access tokens, or screenshots containing those values to the chatbot. Enter payment and verification details only in Rovauto's secure forms and the supported payment provider screen.
