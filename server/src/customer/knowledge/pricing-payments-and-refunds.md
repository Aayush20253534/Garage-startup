# Pricing, Payments, and Refunds

> Customer-help knowledge updated on 28 July 2026.

## Price ranges

Rovauto can show an approved estimated range based on city, service, vehicle brand/model, and fuel type. It is an estimate unless the booking explicitly confirms a final amount.

When no approved range exists, checkout should be blocked rather than showing a made-up price.

## Payment

Use the payment page opened from Rovauto. Payment is confirmed only after the backend verifies the provider order/status. Do not pay again only because a browser redirect was slow; reopen the pending booking or contact support first.

## Wallet

Available wallet balance may be applied according to checkout rules. Wallet debit, payment, refund, and cashback entries are recorded separately. A displayed total should match the booking/payment ledger before completion.

## Garage charges

The garage's internal acceptance fee/wallet is not an extra customer payment unless it is explicitly included in the customer booking summary.

## Refunds

Refund timing depends on booking stage, wallet contribution, and payment provider. A cancelled booking does not always produce an instant bank credit. Check booking/payment history and contact support with the booking code and provider/reference ID.

## Safe payment help

Never send card numbers, UPI PINs, OTPs, passwords, or banking credentials to a garage, worker, chatbot, or support agent. Support may ask for booking/order/reference IDs, not secret credentials.
