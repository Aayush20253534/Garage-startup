# Services, Tracking, Warranty, and Support

> Customer-help knowledge updated on 28 July 2026.

## Service availability

Services can depend on city, category restrictions, vehicle, fuel type, fulfilment method, approved pricing, and garage capability. A service being visible does not always mean it can be booked for every vehicle/location.

## Garage assignment

Rovauto searches garages that can fulfil every selected service for your vehicle and chosen pickup/self-drop method. A garage that serves a brand generally covers all its models only when its service scope is configured for that brand with `All models`.

## Inspection photos and video

Pickup and delivery evidence protects both customer and garage. The current flow requires several clear photos and one video. Review evidence from your booking where available and report mismatches before accepting completion.

## Live tracking

The map can show the latest garage/controller/worker location for supported pickup/delivery stages. Check the last-update time. Browser tracking can pause if the worker closes the page, loses permission, network, or battery.

## Warranty

Completed bookings appear in **Dashboard → Warranty Center**. Warranty cards remain active for 30 days and then show expired. The public Warranty page does not display personal booking warranties.

## Support tickets

Use Support for:

- booking status or garage issue;
- payment/refund issue;
- service quality or warranty claim;
- account problem;
- technical error.

Include the booking code, a concise description, and any displayed reference ID. Attach relevant media only. Do not send passwords, OTPs, or payment secrets.

## Complaints and reviews

Use the complaint flow for a formal issue connected to a booking. Submit a review after the booking experience when the review option is available. Reviews and complaints are separate from warranty/support decisions.
