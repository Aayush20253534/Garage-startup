# Chatbot Privacy and Safe Help

> Customer-help knowledge updated on 28 July 2026.

The Rovauto chatbot can explain customer booking, payment, pickup/self-drop, tracking, warranty, account, service, and support processes using approved help documents and limited account/booking signals.

## Information you should not send

Do not send:

- passwords;
- OTPs or handover OTPs;
- card, UPI PIN, or banking credentials;
- access/session tokens;
- full government identity documents;
- another person's private information;
- secure worker-task links.

Rovauto staff, garage workers, and the chatbot should never ask for your password or payment PIN.

## What the chatbot cannot do

The chatbot cannot:

- confirm payment only from a screenshot;
- override a booking, garage, price, refund, or warranty decision;
- reveal internal admin, infrastructure, or security information;
- expose another customer or garage's data;
- guarantee live worker location when the worker's phone is not updating;
- provide mechanical diagnosis that replaces physical inspection.

## Safe escalation

For a booking/payment/technical issue, use the customer Support page. Include the booking code and any displayed reference ID. Upload only relevant evidence and remove unrelated personal information.

For immediate physical danger or emergency roadside needs, use the available SOS/emergency flow or local emergency services rather than relying only on chatbot messages.
