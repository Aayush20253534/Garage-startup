# Booking Status Help

> Customer-help knowledge updated on 28 July 2026.

## Pending payment

Your booking exists but payment has not been confirmed. Open the pending booking and continue payment. Avoid creating repeated bookings for the same service unless support confirms the previous one is unusable.

## Searching for a garage

Payment is confirmed and Rovauto is contacting compatible nearby garages. Eligibility depends on distance, fulfilment method, vehicle brand/model, selected services, and garage availability. Search may expand automatically.

## Confirmed or garage assigned

A garage has accepted your booking. Review its details and follow pickup or self-drop instructions. `Garage assigned` may appear as a compatibility label for some older booking states.

## In progress

The handover OTP and required vehicle evidence were completed. The garage is working on the booking or the vehicle is in an active collection/return stage.

## Delivered or awaiting acceptance

The garage has submitted delivery/ready-for-pickup evidence. Check the vehicle and complete customer acceptance from the booking page. Contact support before accepting when something is wrong.

## Completed

You accepted the completed service. The booking appears in service history and creates a real Warranty Center card for 30 days.

## Cancelled

The booking was cancelled. Payment and wallet refunds depend on the payment/booking stage and may require provider processing time.

## Expired

The pending payment, garage search, or request window ended without completion. Open the booking for available recovery actions or contact support with the booking code.

## Tracking not updating

The garage worker/controller may need location permission and must keep the tracking page active. A stale map does not automatically mean the booking is cancelled. Check the last update time and contact the garage/support when it remains stale.

## Reference IDs

When an error page shows a reference/request ID, copy it into the support ticket. It helps the technical team locate the server-side event without exposing internal error details.
