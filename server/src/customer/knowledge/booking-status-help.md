# Understanding Booking Status

> Customer-assistant knowledge verified on 25 July 2026.

## Pending Payment

The booking has been saved but garage search has not started. Complete the platform-fee payment from Pending Bookings. If the customer wallet covers the full amount, the booking can proceed without opening Cashfree.

The booking summary identifies whether it is **Pickup & delivery** or **Self drop-off & pickup**. A booking cannot contain both service types.

## Searching Garage

Payment is confirmed and Rovauto is matching eligible garages. The tracker shows the current 5 km, 10 km, or 20 km round. If the full cycle ends without acceptance, the search restarts automatically from 5 km and no extra platform-fee payment is required.

The same garage-search cycle is used for pickup-and-delivery and self drop-off bookings. Garage acceptance is not guaranteed within a specific time.

## Confirmed Or Garage Assigned

A garage has accepted the request. The current acceptance flow normally writes `Confirmed`; `Garage Assigned` may still appear for compatible older records.

For a pickup-and-delivery booking, open the tracker for garage details, the garage's live route when available, and the secure handover OTP. Share the OTP only when the vehicle is physically handed to the assigned garage owner or assigned controller.

For a self drop-off booking, open the tracker for the assigned garage address, directions, contact details, and secure handover OTP. No pickup vehicle will arrive and live pickup tracking is not used. Take the vehicle to the garage and share the OTP only after reaching it.

## In Progress

The handover OTP has been verified, the required pickup or drop-off inspection photos and video were recorded, and service has started. Follow the tracker and garage updates. Normal cancellation is no longer available after service begins; use customer support if there is a problem.

## Delivery Ready Or Ready For Customer Pickup

The garage has completed the work, recorded post-service inspection photos and video, and is waiting for customer confirmation.

For pickup-and-delivery bookings, review the delivered vehicle and photos, enter the final amount paid to the garage, and accept delivery.

For self drop-off bookings, return to the assigned garage, inspect the vehicle and photos, enter the final amount paid, and select **Confirm Vehicle Collection**. The booking is not completed merely because the garage marked it ready.

## Completed

The customer has accepted delivery or confirmed collection and recorded the final amount. The booking appears in service history, can be reviewed, and may show a 30-day warranty card.

## Cancelled

The booking is no longer active. When an eligible paid booking was cancelled before service started, the paid platform-fee amount is credited to the Rovauto wallet. Open payment or wallet history to verify the transaction.

## Expired

The booking or request is no longer active because the allowed flow ended. Open the booking details for the current message and create a new booking or contact support when needed.
